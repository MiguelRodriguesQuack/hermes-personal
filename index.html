Hermes

A personal automation assistant operated by Miguel Rodrigues for his own Google account. This is not a public service and does not offer account registration.

What it does

Hermes supports personal scheduling, calendar summaries, and explicitly requested task capture. Google authorization is used for calendar event access and email sending.

  • Calendar: access to events on owned calendars, including creating, editing, and deleting events when requested.
  • Gmail: sending explicitly requested messages, including task captures to Trello Inbox. The Gmail send permission does not grant mailbox-reading access.

Google data and privacy

Read the privacy policy for the purposes of access, storage, and service-provider processing. This website itself does not request Google login or collect OAuth credentials.
