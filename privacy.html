Privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Hermes automation operated by Miguel Rodrigues. The Google Cloud application is named Hermes. It is for the operator’s own use, not a public service.

Google data accessed and purpose

  • Calendar events: the configured permission, calendar.events.owned, permits access to and management of events on owned calendars. Event details may include titles, times, descriptions, locations, and attendees. They are used for personal scheduling, summaries, reminders, and requested event changes.
  • Gmail sending: gmail.send is used to send messages explicitly requested by the operator, including task titles and descriptions sent to Trello Inbox. This permission does not grant access to read the Gmail inbox.
  • Authorization: Google OAuth credentials are used to maintain the operator’s authorized connection to Google APIs.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the operator-controlled Hermes environment, not in this public website or repository. Task content, tool results, and calendar information may be retained in the assistant’s conversation history, logs, or operator-created notes. There is no fixed automatic deletion period stated here; the operator manages deletion of those local records and any backups.

Processing and sharing

Google receives API requests necessary for the requested operations. Information included in assistant tasks may be processed by the configured AI provider and, when a delivery is requested or configured, services such as Telegram or Trello. An emailed Trello capture passes through Gmail and Trello. These services have their own privacy and retention policies. This policy does not claim all assistant processing stays on the operator’s machine.

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. The intended use is to perform the operator’s personal tasks, not to train an AI model. Provider-side processing and retention depend on the chosen provider and account settings.

Control, revocation, and deletion

The operator can revoke the app’s access through Google Account settings under third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase existing assistant history, backups, or messages already delivered. Those records must be removed separately in the relevant systems. Contact the operator for questions or deletion requests.

This website

These pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or data-submission forms. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process technical request information under GitHub’s policies. Contacting the operator by email sends information through your email provider.
